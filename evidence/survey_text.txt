Economic Overview, Public Finance and Banking
1. Madhya Pradesh has set a target of contributing $550 billion 
in the resolve of the Honourable Prime Minister to make India 
a $5 trillion economy and the state is moving fast in this 
direction. At constant prices, the GSDP of the state this year 
based on 2022-23 (PA) has increased by 7.06%.
2. At constant prices, per capita income has increased from 
Rs.38497 in 2011-12 to Rs. 65023 in 2022-23, showing a growth of 
70 per cent during the period. The per capita income has 
increased by 5.67% this year as compared to last year.
3.  The size of the budget of Madhya Pradesh government in the 
year 2001 was Rs. 16,393 crores in the year 2023 to Rs. 2,47,715 
crore has been done. This increase in the budget is almost 15 
times as compared to the year 2001. The expenditure budget 
of the state in the last year was Rs. 2,17,313 crore (RE) up by 
14% to Rs. 2,47,715 (BE) crores.
4.	 The	 State	has	consistently	followed	fiscal	discipline.	The	debt-
GSDP ratio which was 39.5 per cent in 2005 has come down to 
22.6 per cent in 2020. The capital expenditure of the state 
government is Rs. 37,089 Crore (R.E.) up by 23.18% to Rs. 45,685 
(BE) crores.
5. Between 2018-19 to 2021-22, the state’s own revenue grew at a 
CAGR of 7.94 per cent, while the share in central taxes grew by 
only 0.59 per cent. In 2022-23 (BE), the state’s own revenue 
growth rate stood at 13.32 per cent, while the share in central 
taxes grew by 9.81 per cent over the previous year. This marks 
the beginning of improvement in revenue receipts, thereby 
paving	the	way	for	fiscal	consolidation.
6.	 Moving	 towards	 digitization	 in	 the	 field	 of	 government	
banking business, integration of Madhya Pradesh State

Government’s Integrated Financial Management Information 
System (IFMIS) with Reserve Bank of India’s core banking 
solution ‘e-Kuber’ was implemented on December 2022. . 
Under this arrangement, the withdrawal accounts of all the 54 
treasuries of Madhya Pradesh State have been opened in the 
Reserve Bank of India and are being operated successfully.
7. Keeping in view the increase in the size of the economy, 
priority sector lending has been expanded at the rate of 15.45 
percent CAGR in the state during the period 2005-06 to 2022-
23. Its value increased from Rs 9,437 crore to Rs. 2,15,427 crores. 
In the same sequence, there was an increase of 13.41 percent 
in agriculture loan and M.S.M.E. increased by 30.22 percent.
8. In the state, 59.41 percent of the total advances were given to 
the priority sector, which is more than the national average 
target of 40 percent. While agriculture received 32.2 per cent 
of the total credit, which is higher than the national average 
target of 18 per cent.
9.	 In	 order	to	achieve	the	goals	of	financial	inclusion,	a	total	of	
3.85 crore bank accounts have been opened in the state so far 
under the Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana.
10. Under the Digital District Program, three districts Betul, Indore 
and	 Vidisha	 have	 achieved	 100	 percent	 financial	 inclusion	
digital district status.
11. Unprecedented contribution is being made to the economy by 
self-help groups in Madhya Pradesh, it is noteworthy that a 
large number of women are employed in this. In the year 2012-
13 , 19151 self-help groups were formed in Madhya Pradesh, in 
which 2,34,312 families were included, which has increased to 
4,20,838 by the end of December 2022 (year 2022-23), in which 
47,02,311 families were included. Women Self Help Groups by 
December 2022 through RF (Revolving Funds), CRF (Community 
Revolving Fund), CCL (Cash Credit Limit), Startup Fund up to 
Rs.	Total	financial	support	of	6178.26	crore	has	been	provided.

Agriculture, Food Management & Natural Resources
1.  The state has produced 352.7 lakh tonnes of wheat in 2022-23 
(advance estimates) as compared to 174.8 lakh tonnes in 2013-
14. Similarly, the production of paddy has increased from 53.2 
lakh to 131.8 lakh tonnes during this period.
2. As compared to the year 2020-21, there has been an increase 
of 5.46 percent in the cropped area during the year 2021-22 (A).
3. Compared to the year 2020-21, an increase of 4.16 percent has 
been	 reflected	 in	 the	 production	 of	 total	 crops	 in	 the	 year	
2021-22.
4. The year 2023 has been declared as the ‘Millet Year’ by the 
United Nations General Assembly. It has been called Shri Anna 
by the Government of India. Madhya Pradesh is one of the 
leading producers of Kodo Kutki in India, and is rapidly growing 
in the production and export of Shree Anna. The government 
has started ‘MP State Millet Mission’ and ‘Mukhyamantri 
Kodo-Kutki Kheti Sahayata Yojana’ in the year 2020 to 
encourage the cultivation of Kodo Kutki.
5. In 2021-22, crops of more than 90 lakh farmers were insured 
under the Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana, started to 
protect farmers from losses due to natural calamities.
6.	 Madhya	 Pradesh	is	the	first	state	to	streamline	the	process	of	
Girdawari operations through e_Girdawari project based on 
Artificial	Intelligence/Machine	Learning	(AI/ML)	technology.	In	
this	 sequence,	 measles	 information	 of	 76	 lakh	 beneficiaries	
out of 85 lakh has been registered under PM Kisan.
7. There has been a remarkable change in the irrigation facilities. 
Irrigation capacity has increased by 585 percent in the state, 
it has increased from 7 lakh 68 thousand hectare (in the year 
2003) to more than 45 lakh hectare (in the year 2022), and the 
target is to increase it to 65 lakh hectare by the year 2025.

8. The State Water Policy, 2022 has been drafted by Madhya 
Pradesh through detailed consultations with wide 
stakeholders. The new policy focuses on integrating traditional 
knowledge for water resource conservation and protection.
9. Ramsar wetlands have international importance. Three 
wetlands of Madhya Pradesh (Sirpur wetland and Yashwant 
Sagar in Indore, and Sakhya Sagar in Shivpuri district) have 
been declared as Ramsar sites by the International Convention 
on Wetlands (Ramsar Convention).
10. Fish production in Madhya Pradesh has increased at an 
average rate of 15.39 per cent every year during the period 
2015 to 2022.
11.	 In	 the	 financial	 year	 2021-22,	 the	 state	 ranks	 first	 in	 the	
production of diamond, copper ore and manganese ore, 
second in the production of rock phosphate and limestone, 
and third in the production of coal.

Industry, Trade, Infrastructure and Connectivity
1. Madhya Pradesh Vyapar Samvardhan Parishad (Madhya 
Pradesh Trade Promotion Council) with the objective of 
creating a bridge between government policies and exporter 
interests, focusing on sectors such as food processing, 
textiles, pharmaceuticals, defense and renewable energy, 
with an aim to target a $550 billion economy Council) has 
been established.
2. Madhya Pradesh Startup Policy 2022 and Chief Minister Udyam 
Kranti Yojana have been implemented in the year 2022 to 
promote self-employment, innovative entrepreneurship in 
Madhya Pradesh.
3. Foreign Investment (FDI) is continuously increasing in Madhya 
Pradesh. In the period from April 2021 to March 2022,  
Rs.	 Received	 foreign	 investment	 of	 ₹1560	 crore	 (US$208	
million).
4. Among the land-locked states (Haryana, Rajasthan, Punjab, 
Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh and MP), Madhya Pradesh has 
overtaken Rajasthan in terms of export value and is  in second 
place now followed by Haryana, according to the Export 
Preparedness Index 2021 report.
5.	 Remarkable	 work	 is	 being	 done	 in	 the	 field	 of	 exports	 in	
Madhya	Pradesh.	There	was	a	significant	increase	in	exports	
with an average annual growth rate of 8.4 percent between 
2017 and 2022 and in the year 2021-22, exports are expected to 
reach Rs. 58,407 crore reached.
6. Realizing the importance of tourism in the economy, Madhya 
Pradesh is working on several schemes. End to end support is 
being given to selected tourism related circuits in Madhya 
Pradesh. 50 destinations have been selected as a complete 
package of tourism. The budget allocation for tourism in the 
year 2004-05 was Rs. 11.2 crores, increasing almost 21 times in 
the year 2022-23 to Rs. 244.6 crore has been reached.

7. Compared to the year 2021 in Madhya Pradesh, there has been 
an increase of 122% in the number of tourists in religious 
tourist places in 2022 and 58% increase in the number of 
tourists in other tourist places in 2022.
8. Madhya Pradesh’s power capacity has increased almost 5 
times from 5173 MW in the year 2003 to more than 28000 MW 
in the year 2022. In the last one decade, there has been an 
increase of about 207 percent in the per capita availability of 
electricity in the state. Per capita availability of electricity has 
increased from 570.2 KWH in the year 2012 to 1184.9 KWH in the 
year 2022.
9. Construction and upgradation of roads in the state from 2003 
to 2022 has increased from 44,000 km to more than 4,00,000 
km.
10. Gwalior-Jhansi (NH 75), Mangawan (Rewa)-UP Border (NH 27), 
Seoni-Maharashtra Border (NH 7) etc. have been developed for 
better market access and better connectivity with neighboring 
states.
11. The work of Metro Rail, known as the main source of future 
urban transport, is in progress in Bhopal and Indore, the 
estimated cost of this project is Rs. 14,000 crores.
12. Keeping in view the environment, long-term goals have been 
set by the Madhya Pradesh government. Renewable energy is 
one of the many commitments of the state. Renewable Energy 
Policy 2022 has been implemented to attract investment in 
the renewable energy production sector and renewable energy 
equipment manufacturing sector, to increase electricity 
business and increase employment opportunities, so that 50 
percent of the energy requirements are met by renewable and 
green energy by the year 2030.
13. In Omkareshwar Rs. Work is being done on a 600 MW solar 
power plant with an investment of Rs 3,500 crore. There is a

strengthen the science, technology, research and innovation

Inclusive Social Development 
1. Due to the successful implementation of Ladli Laxmi Yojana in 
Madhya	Pradesh	and	other	efforts,	the	sex	ratio	at	birth	which	
was 927 in the National Family Health Survey-4 (NFHS-4) (2015-
2016) has been increased to 956 inNFHS-5 (2019-2021).
2.  In this sequence, the proportion of literate women in NFHS-3 
(2005-2006) was 44.4, which has increased to 65.4 in NFHS-5 
(2019-2021).
3. About 17,000 women associated with self-help groups have 
won the elections in the panchayat general elections held in 
the year 2022 in Madhya Pradesh. Out of these, 14,378 panchs, 
1907 sarpanchs, 429 sub-sarpanchs, 381 district members and 
46 district panchayat members have been elected, which 
shows the political empowerment and active participation of 
women.
4. In state for the development of Scheduled Tribes the total 
budget allocation of Scheduled Tribes sub plan in 2004-05 is 
Rs. 1,662.01 crore which increased in 2022-23 (BE) to Rs. 
26,940.68 crore, thus an increase of about 1620 percent has 
been observed in this item.
5.	 PESA	 rules	have	been	notified	by	Madhya	Pradesh	on	November	
15, 2022, on the occasion of Tribal Pride Day. The rights of 
water, forest, land, labour, women empowerment and 
strengthening of local traditions have been received

Health and Education
1.  With a view to bring the quality of education in Madhya 
Pradesh to the level of excellence and to make all modern 
facilities	 available	 to	 the	 students,	 in	 the	 first	phase	 under	
the CM RISE scheme, 370 schools of the department have been 
converted into CM RISE schools. 
2. In Madhya Pradesh, cycles are distributed to increase access 
to schools, especially for students living in rural areas, in the 
year 2022-23, cycles have been distributed to about 1 lakh 
students studying in classes 6 to 9. It will be helpful for 
students living in remote areas to continue their education.
3. Girl child education is one of the priorities of Madhya Pradesh. 
207 residential Kasturba Gandhi Girls Schools and additional 
324 girls hostels have been established in the state, 56 
thousand	girls	have	been	benefited	from	these	hostels.
4.	 The	 objective	 of	 Gaon	 Ki	 Beti	 Yojana	 is	 to	 provide	 financial	
assistance to the talented girls of rural areas to encourage 
them towards pursuing higher education. In this scheme, the 
selected	 girl	 student	 is	 paid	 Rs.	 500/-	 per	 month	 (for	 a	
maximum of 10 months) for the undergraduate course. A total 
of	 23,269	 girl	 students	 have	 been	 given	 the	 benefit	 of	 the	
scheme in 2022-23. Similarly, urban girl students have been 
given	the	benefit	of	the	Pratibha	Kiran	Yojana.
5. A total of 10.88 thousand (97.66% achievement) health and 
wellness centers are operational in Madhya Pradesh. In 
addition to these Health and Wellness Centers, 562 AYUSH-
Health and Wellness Centers (362+200) are also being operated 
by the Department of AYUSH.
6.  A high level task force has been constituted by the state 
government to reduce infant mortality rate, maternal mortality 
rate and malnutrition, which is helping the state government 
in	formulating	and	implementing	an	effective	strategy	in	the	
state.

7. According to NFHS-3 (2005-2006) survey, ‘severe malnutrition 
in children under 5 years’ was 12.6% in Madhya Pradesh, which 
has reduced to 6.5% in NFHS-5 (2019-2021) survey |
8.  As per NFHS-3 (2005-2006), institutional delivery was 26% 
which has increased to 90.7% in NFHS-5 (2019-2021).
9. Child Mortality Rate (IMR) in Madhya Pradesh was 70 in NFHS-
3 (year 2005-2006) which has decreased to 41.3 per thousand 
live births in NFHS-5 (year 2019-2021).
10. According to the Registrar General of India (SRS), the Maternal 
Mortality Ratio (MMR) of Madhya Pradesh was 335 in the year 
2004-2006, which has decreased to 173 in the year 2018-2020 
(SRS Bulletin November 2022).

Good Governance and Cultural Economy 
1.	 Many	 efforts	have	been	made	for	the	smooth	and	transparent	
operation of the problems of the general public and activities 
related to governance. Access to services and facilities has 
been increased to the public through e-governance. Public 
Service Guarantee Act, Solution Online, Solution One Day, 
Progress, Cyber Tehsil, MLA Dashboard, Public Hearing, 
Madhya Pradesh E-District Portal, Establishment of Public 
Service Center and Sub Service Center, Chief Minister Helpline 
181, Chief Minister Jan Seva Yojana, Chief Minister Dash Board, 
Self-reliant Madhya Pradesh Portal, Akankshi Development 
Block	Program	etc.	are	major	efforts	in	this	direction.
2. The biggest example of good governance is the Chief Minister’s 
helpline number 181, through this helpline, during the period 
from	 2014	 to	 January	 2023,	 more	 than	 2	 crore	 five	 lakh	
complaints were registered, out of which 98.8 percent have 
been redressed.
3.	 Madhya	 Pradesh	is	the	first	state	in	the	country	to	pass	the	
Public Service Guarantee Act, 2010. Under the Act, till January 
2023,	 696	 services	 of	 48	 departments	 have	 been	 notified	 in	
Madhya Pradesh.
4. Madhya Pradesh e-District Portal was launched in the year 
2012	 for	 electronic	 delivery	 of	 district/sub-district	 level	
services under the National e-Governance Plan (NeGP 2.0) 
under State Mission Mode Projects. By January 2023, 548 
services are being provided online through e-district portal 
and other departmental portals.
5. Capacity Building for Good Governance: Madhya Pradesh State 
Electronics Corporation Limited (MPSeDC) has set up a 
specialized Training Coordination Unit (TCU) to identify IT 
training needs of employees and build capacity of all 
government employees. E-Daksh centers have been set up in 
51 districts of the state. Till January, 2023, about 5.8 lakh 
employees of various departments in Madhya Pradesh have 
been	benefited	through	e-Daksh	Kendras.
