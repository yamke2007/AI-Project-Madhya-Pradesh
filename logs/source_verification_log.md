## Status definitions

- **VERIFIED:** Official source was located and opened during preparation.
- **CORRECTED:** Original candidate URL was wrong or outdated and was replaced with a verified official URL.
- **DROPPED:** Source could not be verified, was inaccessible, duplicated another source without additional value, or was not suitable for the project.

Do not mark a source VERIFIED merely because it appears in a search result. Open it and check the actual page.
